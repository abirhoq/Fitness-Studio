Frequently Asked

We provide mats and towels for all classes, so you don't need to bring those. We recommend comfortable, breathable athletic wear and supportive training shoes (grip socks are great for Pilates and Yoga). Bring a full water bottle and arrive 10–15 minutes early to meet your instructor and get set up. Lockers are available for your belongings.
Absolutely — and you are always welcome here. We specifically designed our Beginner Fitness Bootcamp as a welcoming entry point, and every class includes clear modifications for all fitness levels. Our instructors always offer beginner-friendly alternatives throughout class. We strongly recommend booking our free trial class first so you can get a feel for the studio before committing.
We ask that you cancel your booking at least 6 hours before class start time so your spot can open up for members on the waitlist. Late cancellations and no-shows for drop-in bookings will forfeit the session fee. For monthly members, repeated late cancellations may result in a small fee. We understand that life happens — simply reach out and we'll always do our best to work with you.
Yes! There is a paid public parking garage on Larimer Square, roughly a 2-minute walk from our front door. Street parking is also available on surrounding blocks. We're conveniently located near the 16th Street Mall, making us accessible via Denver's free MallRide bus and multiple RTD light rail stops. We also have a secure bike rack at the studio entrance for cyclists.
We highly recommend booking in advance, as our boutique classes are intentionally kept small (12–18 people max) to ensure quality of instruction. Drop-in members can book up to 24 hours ahead, while monthly and VIP members receive priority booking up to 48 hours before class. Walk-ins are welcome only if space is available on the day. Booking can be done online, via our app, or by calling the studio.
