Studio Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preparing for your first class at Pulse & Form Studio.

Wear breathable athletic clothing that allows complete freedom of movement. For HIIT and Strength classes, cross-training sneakers are required. For Reformer Pilates and Yoga, grip socks or bare feet are practiced. We provide complimentary mats, sweat towels, chilled water refill stations, and luxury locker rooms.
For your initial complimentary trial session, please arrive 15 minutes prior to the start time. This gives our concierge time to register your waiver, introduce you to your coach, set up your equipment or reformer springs, and review any personal movement considerations.
Absolutely. Our classes are structured with regression and progression options for every movement pattern. We also offer dedicated Beginner Bootcamp and Functional Mobility formats designed specifically to teach foundational form before adding load or tempo.
We are situated at 1420 Larimer Street (Sample address). Two-hour metered street parking is available along Larimer and 14th Street. Additionally, validation is provided for up to 90 minutes at the Larimer Square Covered Parking Garage (Sample location) directly across the street.
Because our class sizes are limited to twelve athletes, we maintain an 8-hour cancellation window prior to morning classes and a 4-hour window for evening classes. Cancellations made inside this window forfeit the drop-in credit to preserve spots for waitlisted athletes.
Yes. Members on Unlimited Monthly and VIP plans may freeze their accounts for up to 60 days per calendar year for medical reasons, travel, or work relocations without incurring any pause fees. Simply notify our concierge via email 7 days in advance.
